# odd_sdlc Homeostatic Gap Triage And Intent Renewal Requirements

**Family**: REQ-F-ODDSDLC-*
**Status**: Active
**Category**: Governance
**Carries Forward From**: None
**Authoring Design**: `build_tenants/python/design/HOMEOSTATIC_GAP_TRIAGE_AND_INTENT_RENEWAL.md`, `build_tenants/python/design/QUERY_PLUGIN_CONTRACT.md`

This family defines the homeostatic reverse path for the active `odd_sdlc`
software-domain package.

It turns detected mismatch into lawful observation, semantic triage, route
selection, and when necessary constitutional repricing, without inventing a
second runtime beneath ABG.

### REQ-F-ODDSDLC-033 — odd_sdlc publishes homeostatic observation, triage, and route surfaces

`odd_sdlc` publishes distinct current domain surfaces for observation, triage,
and route state across the active SDLC wave.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- AC-1: meaningful disturbances emit explicit observation state even when full
  triage has not yet completed
- AC-2: observation, triage, route selection, and constitutional repricing
  remain distinct lawful acts rather than one opaque repair step
- AC-3: current domain query can inspect observation, triage, and route state
  directly without inventing a second runtime truth source
- AC-4: ABG remains authoritative for event truth, continuation truth, and
  approval provenance

### REQ-F-ODDSDLC-034 — odd_sdlc classifies gap pressure generically first and totalizes the process boundary

`odd_sdlc` classifies mismatch through a generic process framework before
domain-specific refinement.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- AC-1: triage classifies at least a framework layer and framework condition
  before refining domain-specific gap meaning
- AC-2: every triage attempt resolves to one named process outcome or to an
  explicit `unclassified_gap` or `no_lawful_route` state
- AC-3: no meaningful mismatch falls through into implicit retry, silent
  repair, or undefined state
- AC-4: ambiguity may be carried through the common triage envelope without
  inventing a second independent ambiguity-classification regime

### REQ-F-ODDSDLC-035 — odd_sdlc names lawful re-entry and preserves forward derivation

`odd_sdlc` names the lawful re-entry layer for a detected gap and resumes work
from that layer rather than collapsing everything into immediate code repair.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- AC-1: triage names the lawful re-entry layer at least across code, test,
  design, requirements, product, goals, and intent
- AC-2: chosen route distinguishes fixed-vector repair from declared
  dynamic-family selection and resumes from the named re-entry layer
- AC-3: route and triage publication may preserve current-state signals such as
  preserved structure, unmet pressure, and route eligibility, but they must not
  prescribe builder strategy
- AC-4: when no lawful route exists, the system records that state explicitly
  rather than silently demoting to generic code repair
- AC-5: once a constitutional proposal is lawfully applied, renewed derivation
  must re-read the post-application constitutional surface before forward work
  resumes

### REQ-F-ODDSDLC-036 — constitutional repricing is explicit and gated

When a detected gap cannot be resolved lawfully beneath current Goals or
Intent, `odd_sdlc` opens an explicit constitutional repricing path.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- AC-1: proposed writes to `GOALS.md` or `INTENT.md` are carried as explicit
  constitutional proposals rather than as ambient edits
- AC-2: constitutional change application is governed by declared policy and
  explicit `F_H` approval or declared suppression
- AC-3: `approve`, `approve_with_edits`, `reject`, and `defer` remain distinct
  attributable outcomes
- AC-4: no traversal path may silently apply constitutional change after
  semantic triage alone
- AC-5: `proposal_applied` remains a distinct attributable event from
  proposal authoring and approval intent alone

### REQ-F-ODDSDLC-037 — published analysis and current triage remain fresh, separate, and queryable

`odd_sdlc` keeps workspace-state, published-analysis identity, and current
per-edge triage as separate current surfaces with explicit freshness and
re-entry behavior.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- AC-1: workspace-state, published-analysis identity, and current per-edge
  triage remain distinct current domain surfaces rather than one conflated
  artifact
- AC-2: stale analysis prevents current triage or route truth from being
  served as current state
- AC-3: repeated triage at the same authority basis may supersede the current
  triage result while preserving divergence history
- AC-4: blocked or gated states declare their lawful re-entry trigger,
  including renewed analysis, declared capability, or approval resolution
- AC-5: loopback over an applied proposal emits either `gap_retired` or a new
  attributable `gap_event` rather than leaving the originating gap in
  ambiguous limbo
