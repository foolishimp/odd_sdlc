# odd_method Design

This root carries the shared design surface for `odd_method`.

Use `specification/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md` as the governing method reference
when writing or revising these files.

## Rules

- design records choose the structural how for active requirement truth
- every ADR grounds itself in requirements through explicit `Implements:` lines
- design does not become a second constitution above requirements
- shared realization law may also live under `build_tenants/common/design/`
- tenant-local realization and design live under tenant-local roots in
  `build_tenants/` when such roots are justified
