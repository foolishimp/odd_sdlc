## Design Method Notes

This ticket owns the final proof comparator, not the generation mechanism.
It must remain a projection/evaluator over admitted truth.

Design Module Method obligations:

- derive comparator output from admitted carrier truth
- keep Python baseline as evidence, not architecture authority
- separate implementation optimization from functional omission
- fail closed on missing proof surfaces

## No-Ambiguity Rule

The success claim is:

```text
odd_sdlc.TS matches test35 functionality over data_mapper, but realizes it
correctly through graph functions, typed carriers, ABG runtime truth,
deterministic evaluators, and governed execution evidence.
```

The success claim is not:

```text
odd_sdlc.TS produces something vaguely comparable to test35.
```

Nor is the success claim:

```text
odd_sdlc.TS ran once and accepted its own shallow output.
```
